# Example 03: Media and PR Research

All organizations and names in this example are fictional placeholders.

## Scenario

`Cedarline Mobility` is a fictional company publishing a research report about how families plan regional road trips.

The objective is not to pitch every journalist in the country.

The public-core objective is to identify publications and writers whose actual coverage makes the report relevant enough to justify later human review.

## Mission

```text
Company: Cedarline Mobility
Asset: Original family travel-planning report
Target market: Canada
Goal: Find evidence-backed media prospects
Initial target types: Travel, consumer, regional business, family-lifestyle publications
Possible roles: Editors, journalists, newsletter writers, producers
Tracker: Local CSV
```

## Discovery dimensions

Research may consider:

```text
Publication audience
Editorial section
Writer beat
Recent relevant coverage
Geographic scope
Submission guidance
Timing relevance
```

The Diversity Check should also test whether the best amplification path might include newsletters, specialist communities, data-focused writers, or industry publications rather than only general news outlets.

## Account Research

```text
Prospect C
Verified publication scope: Canadian travel and consumer trends
Verified writer beat: Domestic travel
Recent relevant coverage: Family vacation planning
Unknown: Interest in externally supplied datasets
State: RESEARCHED
```

## Qualification

```text
Status: QUALIFIED
Reason: Audience, beat, geography, recency, and topic align with the report
Confidence: HIGH
```

A publication with only old or generic travel coverage may be `SECONDARY` or `PASS`.

## Prospect Audit

Before shortlist entry, challenge the recommendation:

```text
Is the writer still on the same beat?
Is the relevant coverage current enough?
Are we using one article to infer a broader editorial interest?
Is another publication / writer record actually the same editorial route?
Is there contradictory evidence that submissions are not accepted?
```

Only audited qualified records may become `SHORTLIST_READY`.

## Contact Verification

Prefer supportable professional routes such as:

```text
Official editorial contact
Verified journalist contact published for professional use
Submission form
Publication newsroom route
```

Do not guess private addresses or bypass published submission rules.

## Final Tracker

The public workflow preserves:

```text
Publication / writer identity
Beat and relevance evidence
Recent coverage source
Qualification status
Audit result
Contact-route status
Key uncertainty
Next recommended human action
```

The Open Core does not draft or send the pitch.
