# Example 02: B2B Sales

All organizations and names in this example are fictional placeholders.

## Scenario

`MeridianDesk` is a fictional workflow SaaS company that helps professional-service firms standardize recurring client reporting.

It wants to identify Canadian organizations that appear to have operating structures where this problem may plausibly exist.

The research goal is not to prove pain from the outside. It is to identify evidence-backed accounts worth further business consideration.

## Mission

```text
Company: MeridianDesk
Offer: Workflow and reporting SaaS
Target market: Canada
Initial target hypothesis: Multi-location professional-service firms
Possible relevant functions: Operations, business systems, digital transformation, client services
Tracker: Local CSV
```

## Discovery signals

Useful public signals may include:

```text
Multiple offices or teams
Recurring standardized client deliverables
Complex cross-team service operations
Public transformation initiatives
Relevant business-systems or operations roles
```

A company should not be added merely because it belongs to the target industry.

## Account Research

```text
Prospect B
Verified fact: Operates across multiple offices
Verified fact: Publishes recurring standardized client-facing reports
Observation: Cross-office reporting may require coordination
Hypothesis: A workflow-automation use case is plausible
Unknown: Current software stack and actual manual effort
State: RESEARCHED
```

Do not rewrite the hypothesis as:

```text
Your team wastes hundreds of hours manually creating reports.
```

No public evidence supports that claim.

## Qualification

Evaluate the actual workflow fit, not category membership alone.

Possible result:

```text
Status: QUALIFIED
Reason: Multi-location operations plus recurring standardized deliverables create a supportable workflow-fit hypothesis
Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH
Key uncertainty: Current process and software stack are unknown
```

## Prospect Audit

The skeptical pass should try to weaken the conclusion:

```text
Could the reports be fully automated already?
Is the observed reporting activity actually central to the organization?
Are we confusing a marketing publication with a recurring operational workflow?
Is there contradictory evidence?
Is the prospect duplicated through a parent / subsidiary identity?
```

If those checks materially weaken the use case, downgrade to `SECONDARY` or `HOLD`.

## Contact Verification

For an audited qualified account, research the strongest legitimate professional function and route.

Examples:

```text
Business systems
Operations
Digital transformation
Client operations
Official business inquiry
```

Do not guess private contact information.

## Final Tracker

The final public record should preserve:

```text
Verified facts
Working hypothesis
Counter-evidence
Qualification decision
Audit result
Confidence
Contact-route status
Key uncertainty
Next recommended human action
```

The Open Core stops here. It does not turn the hypothesis into a personalized sales claim or send a message.
