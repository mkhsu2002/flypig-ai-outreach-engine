# Code of Conduct

FlyPig AI Outreach Engine is intended to support professional, constructive open-source collaboration.

## Expected conduct

Participants should:

1. Communicate respectfully and professionally
2. Critique ideas and implementations rather than people
3. Welcome contributors with different technical and business backgrounds
4. Protect private and confidential information
5. Use fictional or appropriately anonymized examples
6. Respect intellectual property, licensing, and attribution
7. Report security or privacy issues through the appropriate channel rather than exposing sensitive details publicly

## Unacceptable conduct

The project does not accept:

1. Harassment or personal attacks
2. Discriminatory or threatening behavior
3. Publishing private personal or business information without permission
4. Deliberately deceptive contributions
5. Spam or promotional abuse of project channels
6. Contributions designed primarily to facilitate impersonation, private-data harvesting, or indiscriminate spam

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or lock content and participation that violates these standards.

Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from project spaces.

## Reporting

For ordinary project disagreements, use the relevant issue or pull request.

For security, privacy, or sensitive conduct reports, follow `SECURITY.md` and avoid posting sensitive details publicly.
