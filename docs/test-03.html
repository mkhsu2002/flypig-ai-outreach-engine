Can FlyPig discover who should care about FlyPig?

Test 03 removed the predefined channel taxonomy. The engine had to infer its own early-adopter ICP, revise the mission, qualify real Canadian organizations, and then face a fresh naked-LLM control run solving the same business problem.

Golden Test 03 · 加拿大 · Dogfooding + Control

FlyPig 能不能自己找出「誰最該在意 FlyPig」？

Test 03 拿掉預先定義好的 channel taxonomy。Engine 必須自己推導 early-adopter ICP、修正 Mission、研究真實加拿大組織，最後再和一個全新的 Naked LLM Control Run 對照。

No obvious category existed.

The problem was not “find AI agencies.” The problem was “who should actually care about an open-source LLM prospect-research process?”

一開始根本沒有明確類別。

題目不是「找 AI agencies」，而是「誰真的應該在意一套 open-source LLM prospect-research process？」

Mission v1

BROAD_ADOPTER_DISCOVERY

Initial hypotheses included international-BD teams, outsourced sales firms, market-entry consultancies, AI/CRM implementers, and public export-support organizations.

初始 hypotheses 同時包含 international-BD teams、outsourced sales、market-entry consultancy、AI/CRM implementer 與 public export-support organizations。

Critical uncertainty

Who gets leverage from the workflow?

誰會從這套 workflow 得到最大槓桿？

Because the product is open-source and workflow-native, the best target might be a multiplier that uses it repeatedly across clients rather than a conventional software buyer.

因為產品是 open-source、workflow-native，最佳對象可能不是一般 software buyer，而是能在多個 clients 上重複使用它的 multiplier。

Discovery found two higher-leverage archetypes.

Repeated market evidence made the broad end-user model less compelling.

Discovery 找到兩種更高槓桿的 archetypes。

反覆出現的市場 evidence 讓「廣泛找一般 end user」變得比較沒有吸引力。

Archetype A

Direct workflow practitioners

Direct workflow practitioners

Market-entry research, partner matching, outsourced prospecting, lead generation, territory research, and international BD are already their daily work.

Market-entry research、partner matching、outsourced prospecting、lead generation、territory research、international BD 本來就是他們每天在做的事。

Archetype B

Implementation multipliers

Implementation multipliers

AI/CRM consultancies already building sales copilots, lead qualification, CRM workflows, guarded automation, enrichment, and source-linked research.

已經替客戶建置 sales copilots、lead qualification、CRM workflows、guarded automation、enrichment、source-linked research 的 AI/CRM consultancies。

Mission v1: BROAD_ADOPTER_DISCOVERY → Mission v2: SERVICE_MULTIPLIER_FIRST

The first-wave thesis became: target people who repeatedly perform the work, and people who already build systems around it.

首波 thesis 變成：先找「反覆執行這類工作的人」，以及「已經替別人建置這類系統的人」。

“AI company” was not enough.

Prospect Audit challenged broad adjacency instead of letting technical capability become product-fit evidence.

「AI 公司」本身不夠。

Prospect Audit 會挑戰 broad adjacency，不允許「技術能力很強」直接變成 product-fit evidence。

Audit signal

CRM / sales workflow

Explicit sales copilots, lead qualification, CRM enrichment, outreach/follow-up automation, or validation increased fit.

明確的 sales copilots、lead qualification、CRM enrichment、outreach/follow-up automation 或 validation evidence 會提高 fit。

Weak signal

Generic AI capability

Generic AI capability

Broad custom software, generic automation, or AI training alone did not prove a recurring prospect-research use case.

Broad custom software、generic automation 或 AI training 本身，不足以證明 recurring prospect-research use case。

Practicality

Could benefit ≠ early prospect

Could benefit ≠ early prospect

Institutional export-support organizations were functionally relevant but were not treated as first-wave founder-led outreach prospects.

Institutional export-support organizations 功能上很相關，但不代表適合作為 founder-led first-wave prospect。

The Naked LLM Control was intentionally strong.

A fresh GPT-5.6 Sol session was given the same underlying business problem but was prohibited from reading FlyPig methodology before finishing its substantive research.

Naked LLM Control 刻意讓它全力發揮。

一個全新的 GPT-5.6 Sol session 收到相同的商業問題，但在完成 substantive research 前，禁止讀取 FlyPig methodology。

Where the control was better

Control 做得更好的地方

It showed broader strategic creativity around accelerators, founder programs, GTM communities, training programs, events, and other amplification channels for an open-source launch.

它在 accelerator、founder program、GTM community、training program、event 與其他 open-source amplification channels 上的策略廣度比原本 FlyPig Test 03 更好。

Where process coverage was weaker

Process coverage 較弱的地方

Its own Execution Notes state that no formal deduplication, mandatory skeptical audit, validation framework, scoring procedure, or state-transition process was performed.

它自己的 Execution Notes 明確記錄：沒有正式 dedup、mandatory skeptical Audit、validation framework、scoring procedure 或 state-transition process。

The control changed FlyPig.

The comparison exposed a real weakness: disciplined research can still be disciplined inside a search space that is too narrow.

Control 反過來改進了 FlyPig。

比較暴露了一個真正弱點：流程再嚴謹，如果 search space 一開始太窄，還是可能嚴謹地錯過重要路徑。

New Core rule: Discovery Diversity Check

Before an ICP or route is allowed to converge, the process now explicitly asks whether direct users, implementation partners, channel partners, communities, training programs, ecosystem amplifiers, institutional routes, or other market-specific roles deserve testing.

在 ICP 或 route 收斂前，Core 現在必須明確檢查：direct users、implementation partners、channel partners、communities、training programs、ecosystem amplifiers、institutional routes 或其他 market-specific roles 是否值得測試。

What the comparison actually supports.

The control result does not support “ChatGPT cannot do this.” It supports a narrower and more practical product claim.

這個比較真正支持什麼。

Control 並不支持「ChatGPT 不會做這件事」，而是支持一個更窄、也更實際的 product claim。

Naked LLM strengths

Strategic creativity + strong one-shot synthesis

Strategic creativity + 強的一次性 synthesis

A capable model can independently form a credible strategy, prioritize prospects, reconsider candidates, and discover channels the framework missed.

能力足夠的模型可以自己形成可信策略、prioritize prospects、重新考慮 candidates，甚至找到 framework 漏掉的 channels。

FlyPig strengths

Persistent, inspectable, required discipline

Persistent、inspectable、required discipline

Canonical dedup, preserved PASS/SECONDARY records, mandatory audit coverage, explicit uncertainty, mission versioning, route state, batch review, and auditable stopping logic are process requirements rather than optional model judgment.

Canonical dedup、保留 PASS/SECONDARY records、mandatory Audit coverage、explicit uncertainty、mission versioning、route state、batch review、auditable stop logic 都是流程要求，不是模型當次想不想做。

The strong control result does not weaken the case for FlyPig. It clarifies it.

Control 表現很好，不是削弱 FlyPig，而是把 FlyPig 的價值講得更清楚。

LLM knows how. FlyPig makes sure the process actually requires it.

Final Test 03 result.

The dogfooding run ended with a sharper launch hypothesis and a smaller audited first-wave set.

Test 03 最終結果。

Dogfooding 最後得到更清楚的 launch hypothesis，以及更小、經過 Audit 的 first-wave set。
