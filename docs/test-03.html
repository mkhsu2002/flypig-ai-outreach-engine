Golden Test 03 · Canada · Dogfooding + Naked LLM Control

Can FlyPig discover who should care about FlyPig — and what happens without FlyPig?

FlyPig 能否自己找出「誰最該在意 FlyPig」？不用 FlyPig 又會怎樣？

The product itself became the test subject. No predefined channel taxonomy was supplied. A separate fresh GPT-5.6 Sol session then solved the same underlying business problem without reading FlyPig methodology.

產品本身成為測試對象，而且沒有預先定義 Channel Taxonomy。之後再使用一個全新的 GPT-5.6 Sol Session，在完全不讀 FlyPig Methodology 的情況下處理同一商業問題。

Abstract

FlyPig revised its own launch mission from BROAD_ADOPTER_DISCOVERY to SERVICE_MULTIPLIER_FIRST, ending with 13 audited SHORTLIST_READY prospects. The naked control independently produced a strong 15-prospect set and showed greater creativity around accelerators, communities, and ecosystem amplifiers. The comparison therefore does not support “LLMs need FlyPig to do good research.” It supports the narrower claim that FlyPig makes critical practices persistent and required.

FlyPig 把自己的 Launch Mission 從 BROAD_ADOPTER_DISCOVERY 改為 SERVICE_MULTIPLIER_FIRST，最後留下 13 個經 Audit 的 SHORTLIST_READY Prospect。裸 LLM Control 也獨立做出很強的 15 家名單，而且在 Accelerator、Community、Ecosystem Amplifier 上更有創意。因此比較並不支持「LLM 不用 FlyPig 就不會做研究」，而是支持更窄的主張：FlyPig 把關鍵做法變成持久且必須執行的流程。

IV

The control improved the Core.

Control 反過來改進了 Core。

A disciplined process can still become disciplined around a search space that is too narrow.

流程再嚴謹，如果 Search Space 一開始太窄，仍可能嚴謹地錯過重要路徑。

New Core rule: Discovery Diversity Check
Before an ICP or route is allowed to converge, the process must test whether materially different direct-user, implementation-partner, channel, community, education, ecosystem, institutional, or market-specific paths deserve consideration.
在 ICP 或 Route 收斂前，流程必須檢查：Direct User、Implementation Partner、Channel、Community、Education、Ecosystem、Institutional 或其他 Market-specific Path 是否值得測試。
V

Final review: interpretation and limitations.

最終檢討：解讀與限制。

The comparison supports a process-reliability claim, not a model-superiority claim.

這個比較支持的是 Process Reliability Claim，不是 Model Superiority Claim。

A strong LLM can produce excellent prospect research on its own. FlyPig attempts to make the practices behind that result repeatable across long, stateful, repeated business-development work.
強大的 LLM 本來就能獨立做出優秀 Prospect Research。FlyPig 嘗試做的，是讓那些好結果背後的重要做法，在長流程、持久狀態、反覆執行的 BD 工作中仍可重複。

Limitations

限制

  • The control was one run, not a large repeated benchmark.
  • The two runs formed different GTM theses, so prospect overlap is not a simple quality metric.
  • Public evidence cannot establish actual adoption appetite.
  • The FlyPig Audit remains same-model role separation, not independent-model review.
  • Control 是單次 Run，不是大型重複 Benchmark。
  • 兩邊形成不同 GTM Thesis，Prospect Overlap 不能直接當品質分數。
  • 公開 Evidence 無法證明真正 Adoption Appetite。
  • FlyPig Audit 仍是同模型 Role Separation，不是 Independent-model Review。
