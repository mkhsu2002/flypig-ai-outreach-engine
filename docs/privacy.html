Privacy notice.

This notice explains how information submitted through the FlyPig AI Outreach Engine website is used when you send a managed-project inquiry.

Who operates this site

FlyPig AI is operated by ICareU Global Trading Ltd., British Columbia, Canada.

Privacy contact: service@flypigai.ca

Information we collect

When you submit a project brief, the form may collect your name, work email, company, website, target market, project objective, offer, target hypothesis, expected prospect volume, budget range, constraints, timeline, and notes.

How we use it

We use submitted information to evaluate the project request, determine whether FlyPig is a suitable fit, communicate with you about the request, and maintain reasonable business records. Requests are stored in a private Cloudflare database before an email notification is sent to our designated business inbox. Successfully sent notification payloads are removed from that database after 30 days during daily maintenance; unresolved requests are retained for review. Delivery records containing request identifiers and one-way recipient hashes are retained to prevent duplicate notifications. Business correspondence in our inbox follows the retention policy below.

Service providers

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Managed-project form notifications are delivered through Resend to FlyPig's designated business inbox. These providers may process technical or submitted data under their own service terms and privacy practices.

No sale of form data

FlyPig does not sell information submitted through this project-inquiry form.

Retention and requests

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary to evaluate and respond to the inquiry and maintain applicable business records, subject to legal obligations. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable.

Browser storage

The site uses local browser storage to remember your language preference. The current site code does not intentionally set advertising cookies.

Please avoid confidential material

Do not submit passwords, confidential customer information, sensitive personal data, private pricing, proprietary BOMs, or other material that is not necessary for initial project evaluation.
