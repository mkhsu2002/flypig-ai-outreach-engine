Managed Prospect Research

You have a product and a market. We find who is worth approaching.

For companies entering a new market, looking for distributors or partners, or building a first-wave B2B target list. FlyPig runs the research, qualification, and audit, then delivers a decision-ready prospect tracker.

01

Projects we are especially good at.

The common thread is simple: you need to know who to approach before your team spends time on outreach.

Market Entry

Enter a new country or industry

Map the market and identify the customer, partner, distributor, integrator, or channel types that matter first.

Partner Discovery

B2B Growth

Build the first target-account list

02

What we do for you.

You provide the business context. We handle the research process.

01

Clarify the target

Understand the offer, market, constraints, and what a useful prospect actually looks like.

02

Research the market

Explore multiple market-entry and prospect paths instead of relying on one initial assumption.

03

Verify and qualify

Research each candidate, remove duplicates, verify role and fit, and audit weak matches.

04

Deliver the tracker
