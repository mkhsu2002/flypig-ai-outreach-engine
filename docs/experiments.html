Validation Experiments · Public Research Record

Three experiments designed to break the process.

三個刻意用來「找出流程弱點」的公開實驗。

These are not three showcase demos. Each experiment targets a different failure mode in long-running prospect research: role drift, a wrong market-entry assumption, and the difference between a strong naked LLM and a process-controlled workflow.

這不是三個展示型 Demo。每個實驗都針對不同的長流程 Prospect Research 失敗模式：角色漂移、錯誤的市場進入假設，以及強大裸 LLM 與受流程控制研究框架之間的差異。

The purpose is not to prove that FlyPig is smarter than ChatGPT. The purpose is to test whether important research disciplines remain explicit, persistent, and auditable when the mission becomes long and messy.

目的不是證明 FlyPig 比 ChatGPT 聰明，而是檢驗：當任務變長、資料變亂、判斷開始累積時，重要的研究紀律能否持續被明確執行、保存與稽核。

01

Japan outdoor market: can role definitions survive a long search?

日本戶外市場：長流程研究後，商業角色還能不漂移嗎？

Question & process

A privacy-safe Canadian outdoor-accessories brand explored Japan. The process had to distinguish importer, distributor, retailer, and manufacturer roles; deduplicate repeated identities; audit every qualified record; and keep commercial fit separate from contact-route quality.

以匿名化加拿大戶外配件品牌探索日本。流程必須持續區分 Importer、Distributor、Retailer、Manufacturer，處理重複公司、對每個 Qualified Prospect 進行反向 Audit，並把 Commercial Fit 與 Contact Route 分開。

Result & review

44 material mentions became 34 canonical candidates; 20 were fully researched; 15 initially qualified; 3 were downgraded by Audit; 12 remained SHORTLIST_READY. The lesson: category relevance and contactability cannot substitute for verified commercial-role evidence.

44 個 Material Mentions 收斂為 34 個 Canonical Candidates；完整研究 20 家；15 家初步 Qualified；Audit 再降級 3 家；最終 12 家 SHORTLIST_READY。核心心得：類別相關、品牌熟悉或「找得到人」都不能取代真正的商業角色證據。

02

Germany industrial water: what if the user's original search model is wrong?

德國工業水質：如果一開始「找 Distributor」這個假設就太窄呢？

Question & process

The mission started DISTRIBUTOR_FIRST. Repeated evidence surfaced instrumentation integrators, water/wastewater automation firms, and turnkey engineering companies that influence specification and commissioning. The workflow had to decide whether to preserve or revise the original mission.

任務一開始採用 DISTRIBUTOR_FIRST，但研究反覆出現 Instrumentation Integrator、Water/Wastewater Automation Firm 與 Turnkey Engineering Company。流程必須判斷：應繼續照原要求找，還是根據市場證據正式改寫 Mission。

Result & review

The mission formally changed to CHANNEL_PLUS_INTEGRATION. Of 12 initial QUALIFIED prospects, 5 were downgraded during Audit and 7 remained SHORTLIST_READY. The lesson: disciplined research should be able to challenge the initial route-to-market assumption rather than automate it blindly.

Mission 正式改為 CHANNEL_PLUS_INTEGRATION。12 家初步 Qualified 中，5 家在 Audit 後降級，7 家留下。核心心得：好的研究流程不只執行原始指令，也必須有能力在市場證據足夠時挑戰原本的 Route-to-market 假設。

03

FlyPig on FlyPig: does the framework add anything when the naked LLM is already very good?

FlyPig 找 FlyPig 的客戶：當裸 LLM 已經很強，這個框架還增加了什麼？

Question & process

FlyPig used its own workflow to find early adopters in Canada, then a fresh capable LLM solved the same underlying problem without reading FlyPig methodology. This created a deliberately difficult control: the baseline was allowed to be strong.

FlyPig 用自己找加拿大第一批可能採用者，再另外讓一個全新的高階 LLM 在完全不讀 FlyPig Methodology 的情況下處理同一商業問題。這是一個刻意把 Control 設得很強的比較。

Result & review

FlyPig produced 13 audited SHORTLIST_READY prospects. The naked LLM independently produced a strong 15-prospect set and showed broader strategic creativity. That result changed the Core: a Discovery Diversity Check was added. The lesson is narrower and stronger — FlyPig is about process reliability, not model superiority.

FlyPig 最終留下 13 個經 Audit 的 SHORTLIST_READY；裸 LLM 也做出很強的 15 家名單，而且策略探索更廣。這個結果反過來促使 Core 新增 Discovery Diversity Check。核心心得因此更精準：FlyPig 要解決的是 Process Reliability，不是宣稱模型比較聰明。

WHY

Why publish the failures and corrections?

為什麼連失敗與修正也公開？

A prospect-research framework is only useful if it can expose where its own assumptions fail. The experiments therefore preserve downgrades, control strengths, limitations, and process changes rather than presenting only successful shortlists.

Prospect Research Framework 真正有價值的前提，是它必須能暴露自己的錯誤假設。因此這些實驗不只展示成功名單，也保留 Downgrade、Control 的強項、限制，以及因實驗結果而修改 Core 的過程。
