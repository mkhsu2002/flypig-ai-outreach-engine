Validation Experiments

Three tests. Three different questions.

We did not repeat the same demo three times. Each experiment was designed to expose a different failure mode in AI-assisted prospect research.

Can the system keep business roles accurate across a long search?

A Canadian outdoor-accessories brand explored Japan. The test focused on importer, distributor, retailer, and manufacturer classification, deduplication, skeptical audit, and contact-route separation.
