FlyPig Research System / Research Note II
Golden Test 02 · Germany · Industrial Water Monitoring

When “find distributors” is the wrong search model.

當「找 Distributor」本身就是太窄的搜尋模型。

This case tested whether evidence could revise the user's original route-to-market assumption rather than merely automate it.

此案例測試：當市場 Evidence 與使用者最初 Route-to-market 假設衝突時，流程能否修正 Mission，而不是只把原始要求自動化。

Abstract

Broad German and English discovery repeatedly surfaced not only distributors, but multi-brand instrumentation integrators, water/wastewater automation firms, and turnkey engineering companies that specify and commission field instrumentation. FlyPig formally revised the mission, then used Prospect Audit to downgrade five of twelve initial QUALIFIED decisions.

德文與英文 Discovery 反覆找到的不只是 Distributor，而是 Multi-brand Instrumentation Integrator、Water / Wastewater Automation Firm，以及會指定、採購與 Commission Field Instrumentation 的 Turnkey Engineering Company。FlyPig 因此正式修訂 Mission，之後 Prospect Audit 又將 12 個 Initial QUALIFIED 中的 5 個降級。

I

The initial assumption was intentionally provisional.

初始假設刻意只被視為 Provisional。

The fictional supplier offered modular online pH, conductivity, dissolved-oxygen, and turbidity monitoring equipment.

虛構供應商提供 Online pH、Conductivity、Dissolved Oxygen、Turbidity 等模組化監測設備。

II

Discovery found three commercially distinct routes.

Discovery 找到三種不同的商業路徑。

The route to deployment may be controlled by firms that specify and integrate instruments rather than merely stock and resell them.

真正影響設備進入專案的，不一定只是 Stock-and-resell Distributor，也可能是會 Specify、Integrate、Commission 儀器的工程商。

Route A

Distributor + System Integrator

Distributor + System Integrator

Multi-brand instrumentation businesses combining procurement, installation, calibration, and controls integration.

同時具備 Multi-brand Instrument Procurement、Installation、Calibration 與 Control Integration 的業者。

Route B

Water / wastewater automation integrator

Water / Wastewater Automation Integrator

MSR, PLC/SCADA, commissioning, and field-technology integration can make these firms real gatekeepers.

MSR、PLC/SCADA、Commissioning 與 Field Technology Integration 讓這類公司可能成為真正 Gatekeeper。

Route C

Turnkey engineering / EPC

Turnkey Engineering / EPC

Project builders may specify or procure third-party field instrumentation inside complete industrial-water systems.

完整工業水處理專案的工程商，可能會直接 Specify 或 Procure Third-party Field Instrumentation。

III

The false-positive test: technical relevance is not commercial fit.

False Positive 測試：Technical Relevance 不等於 Commercial Fit。

The final batch deliberately included highly relevant water-analysis manufacturers.

最後一批刻意加入技術高度相關的 Water-analysis Manufacturer。

A company can match every technical keyword and still be the wrong prospect because it manufactures the competing measurement stack itself.

一家公司可以符合幾乎所有 Technical Keyword，卻仍然不是合適 Prospect，因為它自己就在製造重疊的 Measurement Stack。

Several such firms were preserved as PASS rather than silently discarded, so later campaigns can distinguish competitor/ecosystem references from channel prospects.

這些公司被保留為 PASS，而不是直接刪除，讓後續 Campaign 能區分 Competitor / Ecosystem Reference 與真正 Channel Prospect。

V

Interpretation and limitations.

解讀與限制。

Test 02 is evidence that the process can change the market model, not evidence that shortlisted firms will sign.

Test 02 證明流程可以改變 Market Model，不代表 Shortlisted Firm 一定會簽約。

FlyPig did not “find better distributors.” It discovered that distribution was only one part of the German route-to-market structure.
FlyPig 並不是「找到更好的 Distributor」，而是發現 Distributor 只是德國 Route-to-market Structure 的其中一部分。

Limitations

限制

  • Pricing, EU certification, service, and stocking requirements were incomplete.
  • Public evidence cannot prove supplier onboarding appetite.
  • Large EPC firms may have opaque procurement processes.
  • SHORTLIST_READY is a next-step research judgment, not purchase intent.
  • Pricing、EU Certification、Service、Stocking Requirements 並不完整。
  • 公開 Evidence 無法證明 Supplier Onboarding Appetite。
  • 大型 EPC 的 Procurement Process 可能不透明。
  • SHORTLIST_READY 是 Next-step Research Judgment，不是 Purchase Intent。
