Experiment 02 · Germany Industrial Water

What if “find distributors” is the wrong search model?

如果「找 Distributor」本身就是太窄的搜尋模型？

The supplier wanted to enter Germany with industrial water-quality monitoring equipment. The experiment tested whether the process would simply obey “find distributors,” or revise the market-entry model when evidence pointed elsewhere.

供應商希望以工業水質監測設備進入德國。這個實驗要測的是：系統會不會只照著「找 Distributor」執行，還是能在市場證據指向其他路徑時正式修正 Mission。

01

Question and setup

題目與設定

Mission v1

DISTRIBUTOR_FIRST

The original assumption was that distributors would be the primary route into the German market.

原始假設是：進入德國市場的主要路徑應該是 Distributor。

Trigger

Repeated ecosystem evidence

反覆出現的生態系證據

Searches repeatedly surfaced instrumentation integrators, water/wastewater automation firms, and turnkey engineering companies that specify and commission field equipment.

搜尋反覆找到 Instrumentation Integrator、Water/Wastewater Automation Firm 與會 Specify / Commission 現場設備的 Turnkey Engineering Company。

02

Process

過程

01

Start with distributors

先依 Distributor 假設搜尋

Treat the client's initial model as a hypothesis, not a fixed truth.

把客戶的原始想法當作 Hypothesis，而不是不可改變的答案。

02

Map adjacent routes

辨識相鄰市場路徑

Observe which firms actually influence specification, integration, procurement, and commissioning.

觀察實際影響 Specification、Integration、Procurement、Commissioning 的公司。

03

Revise the mission

正式修正 Mission

Change the route-to-market model when repeated evidence supports a broader structure.

當證據持續支持更廣的市場結構時，正式改寫研究方向。

04

Audit commercial fit

Audit 商業適配

Separate true channel candidates from technically relevant manufacturers and weak-fit companies.

把真正 Channel Candidate 與只是技術相關的 Manufacturer、弱適配公司分開。

Audit downgraded 5 of 12 initial QUALIFIED candidates. Several technically relevant manufacturers were kept as PASS because they were the wrong commercial role.

Audit 把 12 家 Initial QUALIFIED 中的 5 家降級；另外一些技術上高度相關的 Manufacturer 因商業角色不對，被保留為 PASS，而不是硬塞進 Prospect 名單。

04

What the experiment changed

實驗帶來的修正

Route A

Distributor + System Integrator

Some firms combine product sourcing with installation, calibration, and control integration.

有些公司同時具備產品採購、安裝、Calibration 與 Control Integration。

Route B

Water / wastewater automation integrator

Water / Wastewater Automation Integrator

PLC/SCADA and commissioning roles can make these firms real gatekeepers to deployment.

PLC/SCADA 與 Commissioning 能力讓這類公司成為實際導入的 Gatekeeper。

Route C

Turnkey Engineering / EPC

Project builders may specify and procure third-party instrumentation inside complete systems.

大型工程商可能在完整系統中直接 Specify 與 Procure 第三方儀器。

05

Final review

最終心得與限制

The best outcome was not “better distributors.” It was discovering that distributors were only one part of the actual route-to-market structure.
最重要的成果不是「找到更好的 Distributor」，而是發現 Distributor 其實只是德國 Route-to-market Structure 的其中一部分。

Limitations

限制

  • Pricing, EU certification, service, and stocking requirements were incomplete.
  • Public evidence cannot prove supplier-onboarding appetite.
  • Large EPC procurement processes may remain opaque.
  • SHORTLIST_READY is not purchase intent.
  • Pricing、EU Certification、Service 與 Stocking 條件不完整。
  • 公開資訊無法證明對方真的願意 Onboard 新供應商。
  • 大型 EPC 的採購流程可能不透明。
  • SHORTLIST_READY 不等於 Purchase Intent。
