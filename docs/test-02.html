Golden Test 02 · Germany industrial water monitoring

Can evidence force the mission to change?

Test 02 started with a conventional distributor-first assumption. The market evidence repeatedly pointed to system integrators and engineering firms, forcing a formal Mission Revision rather than silently broadening the search.

Golden Test 02 · 德國工業水質監測

當市場證據不同，Mission 會不會真的改？

Test 02 從最常見的 distributor-first 假設開始，但市場 evidence 一再指向 system integrator 與 engineering firms，因此流程必須正式 Mission Revision，而不是偷偷把 search criteria 放寬。

The starting assumption.

The fictional supplier made online industrial water-quality sensors and normally worked through distributors.

一開始的假設。

虛構供應商提供工業用 online water-quality sensors，過去習慣透過 distributors 銷售。

Mission v1

DISTRIBUTOR_FIRST

Initial targets: German distributors, measurement-equipment wholesalers, and technical distributors serving water/wastewater customers.

初始 targets：德國 distributors、measurement-equipment wholesalers，以及服務水／廢水客戶的 technical distributors。

Important limitation

Channel fit, not sales prediction

研究 channel fit，不預測成交

Exact SKUs, certifications, pricing, service model, and logistics were unknown, so the run could evaluate organization fit but not claim a final buying outcome.

SKU、認證、價格、service model、物流都未知，因此只能判斷 organization / channel fit，不能宣稱最終 buying outcome。

Discovery contradicted the first model.

Three recurring organization types appeared in current German market evidence.

Discovery 開始反駁原本模型。

德國市場的目前 evidence 反覆出現三種 organization types。

Pattern A

Distributor + integrator hybrids

Distributor + integrator 混合型

Companies that distribute instrumentation but also install, calibrate, commission, or integrate systems.

不只賣 instrumentation，也進行 installation、calibration、commissioning 或 system integration。

Pattern B

Water / wastewater automation integrators

Water / wastewater automation integrators

Firms designing MSR, PLC/SCADA, and field-instrument integration for plants.

負責 MSR、PLC/SCADA 與 field-instrument integration 的系統整合商。

Pattern C

Turnkey water engineering firms

Turnkey water engineering firms

Engineering / EPC companies that specify and integrate third-party instrumentation inside complete projects.

在完整工程專案中 specification、採購並整合第三方 instrumentation 的 engineering / EPC 公司。

Mission v1: DISTRIBUTOR_FIRST → Mission v2: CHANNEL_PLUS_INTEGRATION

The process did not conclude that distributors were wrong. It concluded that distributor-only was incomplete.

流程不是判定 distributor 錯了，而是判定「只找 distributor」這個 market model 不完整。

Why Mission Revision matters.

A weak automation system would keep executing the original request more efficiently. FlyPig requires the mission itself to be revisable.

為什麼 Mission Revision 很重要。

弱的 automation system 只會更有效率地繼續執行原始要求；FlyPig 要求 Mission 本身可以被 evidence 修正。

01

Treat the user's channel model as a hypothesis.

把使用者的 channel model 當成 hypothesis。

“We normally use distributors” is useful context, not proof that Germany should use the same route.

「我們平常用 distributor」是重要 context，但不是德國也一定適用的證據。

02

Let discovery surface alternate gatekeepers.

讓 Discovery 找出其他真正 gatekeepers。

The strongest project access could come from companies that specify, integrate, commission, and service field instruments.

真正掌握 project access 的，可能是負責 specification、integration、commissioning、service 的公司。

03

Version the mission explicitly.

正式 versioning Mission。

The target model changed from distributor-first to channel-plus-integration rather than silently redefining what “distributor” meant.

流程不是偷偷擴張「distributor」的定義，而是正式從 distributor-first 改成 channel-plus-integration。

Technical relevance is not commercial prospect fit.

The last batch deliberately tested high-keyword-overlap companies.

Technical relevance 不等於 commercial prospect fit。

最後一批刻意放入高度關鍵字相關的公司做 false-positive test。

Good prospect pattern

好的 prospect pattern

A distributor/integrator or engineering firm with evidence that it selects, procures, integrates, or supports third-party field instrumentation.

有 evidence 顯示會選型、採購、整合或支援第三方 field instrumentation 的 distributor/integrator 或 engineering firm。

High relevance, wrong role

高度相關，但角色錯誤

A German manufacturer of its own pH, conductivity, turbidity, or integrated water-analysis stack may match every technical keyword and still be a poor channel prospect.

自己製造 pH、conductivity、turbidity 或整套 water-analysis stack 的德國 OEM，即使每個技術關鍵字都命中，也可能完全不是正確 channel prospect。

Prospect Audit changed 5 of 12 initial QUALIFIED decisions.

Audit did not merely restate the qualification. It challenged channel openness, supplier diversity, instrumentation sourcing, and incumbent relationships.

12 個 initial QUALIFIED 中，5 個被 Audit 改判。

Audit 不是重述 Qualification，而是重新挑戰 channel openness、supplier diversity、instrumentation sourcing 與 incumbent relationships。

Proprietary stack risk

Proprietary stack risk

Integration capability was real, but a strong own measurement platform reduced the external-brand adoption hypothesis.

Integration 能力是真的，但強大的自有 measurement platform 會削弱採用外部品牌的 hypothesis。

Automation ≠ analyser sourcing

Automation ≠ analyser sourcing

Strong SCADA / automation evidence did not automatically prove meaningful responsibility for water-quality sensor selection.

很強的 SCADA / automation evidence，不代表它真的負責 water-quality sensor selection。

Incumbent-channel friction

Incumbent-channel friction

A real distributor could still be second-wave if incumbent brand relationships made first-wave adoption less plausible.

即使真的有 distributor role，如果 incumbent brand relationship 太強，仍可能被降到 second-wave。

Final result.

Test 02 demonstrated that the process can challenge the user's initial market-entry model instead of simply automating it.

最終結果。

Test 02 證明流程可以挑戰使用者原本的 market-entry model，而不是只把原始想法自動化。
