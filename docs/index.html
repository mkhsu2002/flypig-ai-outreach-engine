01

The hard part is not building a list. It is knowing who is actually worth contacting.

難的不是列出公司，而是判斷誰真的值得接觸。

Pain 01

You know the market, but not the first targets

知道市場有機會，卻不知道第一批該找誰

Entering a new country or industry often starts with an unclear question: customer, distributor, integrator, partner, or someone else?

進入新國家或新產業時，最常見的問題不是「有沒有公司」，而是到底該找客戶、經銷商、整合商、合作夥伴，還是其他角色。

Pain 02

Generic lead lists create noise

一般名單很多，但真正能用的很少

Keyword matches and directory results can look relevant while having the wrong business role, weak fit, or no practical route to outreach.

關鍵字相符不代表商業角色正確。很多名單看起來相關，實際上並不適合開發。

Pain 03

Research quality drops as the job gets bigger

研究一多，判斷就容易開始漂移

Across dozens of companies, assumptions change, duplicates return, and earlier decisions get lost. FlyPig keeps the work structured from start to finish.

當研究擴大到數十家公司，目標容易漂移、重複公司再次出現、先前判斷也容易遺失。FlyPig 的工作就是把整個過程管住。

01

Describe the goal

說明你的目標

Product, market, target geography, constraints, and what you hope to achieve.

產品、目標市場、地區、限制條件，以及你真正想達成的商業目的。

02

Explore the market

探索市場結構

Research different customer, partner, channel, and ecosystem paths instead of forcing one assumption.

不只照著原始假設硬找，而是探索客戶、通路、合作夥伴與其他可能路徑。

03

Verify and qualify

查證與篩選

Research each candidate, remove duplicates, check commercial role, and challenge weak matches.

逐一研究候選對象、去重、確認商業角色，並把證據不足的對象降級或排除。

04

Deliver a usable tracker

交付可直接使用的 Tracker

Keep the evidence, status, contact route, uncertainty, and next action in one persistent record.

把證據、資格狀態、聯絡路徑、不確定性與下一步統一保存在一份 Tracker 中。

04

Tested on three different business-development problems.

我們用三種不同的商務開發問題實際測試。

Each experiment tests a different weakness: role drift, a wrong market-entry assumption, and whether a strong naked LLM still benefits from a structured process.

三個實驗分別測試：角色漂移、錯誤的市場進入假設，以及當裸 LLM 已經很強時，流程框架還能增加什麼價值。

Experiment 01 · Japan

Can roles stay accurate across a long search?

長流程研究後，商業角色還能保持準確嗎？

Japan outdoor market. 44 candidate mentions became 34 canonical candidates, 20 were fully researched, and 12 survived final audit.

日本戶外市場。44 個候選提及收斂成 34 個 Canonical Candidates，完整研究 20 家，最終 12 家通過 Audit。
