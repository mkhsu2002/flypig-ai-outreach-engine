01

The real bottleneck is not finding companies. It is knowing which ones deserve your sales team's time.

真正的瓶頸不是「找得到公司」，而是知道哪些公司值得你的業務花時間。

Problem 01

You want to enter a market, but the target role is unclear

想進一個市場，卻連該找哪種角色都不確定

Customer? Distributor? Importer? Integrator? Strategic partner? The wrong assumption can waste the entire campaign.

客戶？Distributor？Importer？Integrator？Strategic Partner？一開始找錯角色，後面的開發工作都可能白做。

Problem 02

Lead lists look relevant but collapse under inspection

名單看起來很多，實際查下去卻不適合

Category similarity and keywords are not enough. A company can look perfect and still have the wrong commercial role.

品類相似、關鍵字符合都不代表它真的適合。很多公司看起來很像目標，商業角色其實完全不對。

Problem 03

Manual research does not scale cleanly

人工研究一多，就開始重複、遺漏與失焦

As the list grows, duplicates return, earlier decisions disappear, and qualification standards drift.

當名單擴大，重複公司會重新出現、先前判斷會遺失，Qualification 標準也容易慢慢漂移。

04

How FlyPig runs the research.

FlyPig 怎麼把模糊需求變成可執行名單。

01

Understand the business goal

理解商業目標

Product, target market, geography, constraints, and what success would look like.

產品、目標市場、地區、限制條件，以及你希望達成的結果。

02

Explore multiple routes

探索不同市場路徑

Test customer, partner, distributor, integrator, and ecosystem hypotheses instead of locking into one assumption too early.

同時測試 Customer、Partner、Distributor、Integrator 與其他 Ecosystem 路徑，不過早鎖定單一假設。

03

Research and challenge every candidate

逐一研究，並重新挑戰判斷

Verify role and fit, remove duplicates, preserve evidence, and audit weak or overconfident matches.

查證商業角色與 Fit、去重、保留證據，並 Audit 證據不足或過度樂觀的判斷。

04

Deliver the qualified tracker

交付 Qualified Prospect Tracker

Your team gets the researched prospect set, evidence, contact route, uncertainty, and next action in one place.

研究過的 Prospect、Evidence、Contact Route、不確定性與下一步都集中在同一份 Tracker。
