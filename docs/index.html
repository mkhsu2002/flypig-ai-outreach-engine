Prospect research that keeps its discipline when the conversation gets long.

FlyPig AI Outreach Engine does not try to make the LLM smarter. It turns critical prospect-research practices into required operating discipline: deduplication, evidence checks, search diversity, skeptical audit, persistent state, and explicit stopping rules.

Why FlyPig exists.

Naked LLM research was often surprisingly good. The failure mode appeared later: dozens of turns into the same mission.

為什麼會有 FlyPig。

裸 LLM 的研究成果往往出乎意料地好。真正的問題通常出現在同一任務進行了幾十輪之後。

01 · Drift

The target quietly changes

目標悄悄改變

A distributor search gradually admits retailers, manufacturers, and adjacent companies without an explicit mission decision.

原本找 distributor，後來 retailer、manufacturer、相鄰公司慢慢都被放進來，但 Mission 並沒有正式改變。

02 · Memory

Rejected research disappears

排除過的研究消失

Weak accounts are rediscovered because the prior decision lived only in conversation instead of one canonical Tracker.

弱 prospect 因為先前決策只存在對話中，而沒有保存在 canonical Tracker，之後又被重新找到。

03 · Downstream risk

Good writing, wrong context

文字很好，商業語境卻錯了

Once business-role research drifts, later communication can sound polished while speaking to the recipient as the wrong kind of organization.

一旦 business role 研究飄移，後續文字即使很流暢，也可能用完全錯誤的角色假設與對方溝通。

What FlyPig makes mandatory.

The product thesis is not “the LLM does not know.” It is “the process should not depend on whether the model remembers every good practice this time.”

FlyPig 強制要求什麼。

核心命題不是「LLM 不知道怎麼做」，而是「不能把流程品質賭在模型這一次剛好有沒有記得每個好習慣」。

Canonical Tracker

One persistent campaign record, including rejected and lower-priority research.

一份持續更新的 campaign record，包含被排除與低優先的研究結果。

Dedup before insertion

Aliases, domains, parent entities, and related identities are checked before a new row exists.

Alias、domain、parent entity 與 related identity 在新建 row 前先檢查。

Evidence discipline

Material role and fit decisions need supportable current evidence.

重要的 role 與 fit 判斷必須有可支持的目前 evidence。

Discovery Diversity

Before convergence, test whether another organization type or route could satisfy the objective better.

Search space 收斂前，先檢查是否有另一種 organization type 或 route 更能達成目標。

Skeptical Prospect Audit

Every QUALIFIED prospect is challenged under the question: what would make this decision wrong?

每一個 QUALIFIED prospect 都必須接受「什麼證據會證明剛才判斷是錯的？」的反向 Audit。

Batch review + stop rules

Long runs periodically review drift and stop when marginal discovery value falls.

長流程定期檢查 drift，並在新增搜尋的邊際價值下降時明確停止。

Validated on three different failure modes.

Each Golden Test was designed to stress a different part of the process rather than repeat the same demo three times.

三個 Test，驗證三種不同失敗模式。

三組 Golden Test 不是重複同一個 Demo，而是刻意壓測流程中不同的風險。

Golden Test 01 · Japan

Role classification + skeptical audit

角色分類 + skeptical audit

Could the system keep retailer, importer, distributor, manufacturer, and market-entry roles separate across a long run?

長流程裡，系統能否持續區分 retailer、importer、distributor、manufacturer 與 market-entry partner？
Golden Test 02 · Germany

Mission Revision under evidence

Evidence 驅動 Mission Revision

Could research overturn a distributor-first assumption when system integrators and engineering firms were the stronger route?

當市場 evidence 顯示 system integrator 與工程商更重要時，流程能否修正原本 distributor-first 的假設？
Golden Test 03 · Canada

ICP discovery + dogfooding + control

ICP Discovery + Dogfooding + Control

Could FlyPig infer who should care about FlyPig itself — and what happens when a fresh naked LLM solves the same problem?

FlyPig 能不能自己找出「誰最該在意 FlyPig」？而全新的裸 LLM 做同一題時又會發生什麼？

The control result was strong. That is the point.

A fresh GPT-5.6 Sol run without FlyPig produced a useful 15-prospect Canadian strategy. FlyPig should not compete with the intelligence of the model.

Control Run 很強，這反而才是重點。

全新的 GPT-5.6 Sol 在不用 FlyPig 的情況下，也做出了實用的 15 家加拿大 prospect 策略。FlyPig 不應該和底層模型的 intelligence 競爭。

Naked LLM

Excellent one-shot judgment

優秀的一次性判斷

Strong synthesis, natural prioritization, and broader creativity around accelerators, communities, and amplification channels.

很好的 synthesis、自然 prioritization，而且在 accelerator、community、amplification channel 上甚至比 FlyPig Test 03 更有創意。

FlyPig Process

Required operating discipline

強制 operating discipline

Formal dedup, persistent rejected records, mandatory audit coverage, mission versions, route state, batch review, and explicit stopping logic.

正式 Dedup、保留 rejected records、mandatory Audit coverage、mission version、route state、batch review 與明確 stop logic。

從正常的商業想法開始。
