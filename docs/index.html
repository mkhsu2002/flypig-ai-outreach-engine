Frequently asked questions

What is FlyPig AI Outreach Engine?

The Open Core is an open-source set of LLM-readable Skills, process rules, schemas and templates for prospect research and qualification. It runs inside a capable LLM or Agent with current web research access and maintains one Qualified Prospect Tracker.

How is the Open Core different from the managed service?

You can use the Apache-2.0 Open Core yourself without installing a FlyPig server. The managed service is a separate commissioned engagement in which FlyPig runs the research, qualification and audit, then delivers an evidence-backed prospect tracker. Scope and terms are discussed after reviewing your brief.

Does the public Open Core send cold emails or automate LinkedIn?

No. The public workflow ends at the Qualified Prospect Tracker. Message preparation, email sending, LinkedIn automation, reply tracking and follow-up execution are outside the public Open Core.

What do the three published experiments demonstrate?

They examine commercial-role classification in Japan, evidence-led mission revision in Germany, and early-adopter research in Canada with a fresh LLM control. These are published case studies, not proof of model superiority or guaranteed replies, sales or adoption. Each experiment links to its public validation artifacts.
