FlyPig Research System / Experiments / Research Note I
Golden Test 01 · Japan Outdoor Market · Regression v2

Can one LLM keep commercial roles distinct across a long market-entry study?

一個 LLM 能否在長流程市場研究中，持續正確區分商業角色？

A privacy-safe Canadian outdoor-accessories brand explored Japan. The test focused on canonical dedup, importer/distributor/retailer/manufacturer classification, skeptical audit, and contact-policy separation.

以 Privacy-safe 的加拿大戶外配件品牌探索日本市場。此測試聚焦 Canonical Dedup、Importer / Distributor / Retailer / Manufacturer 角色分類、Skeptical Audit，以及 Contact Policy 與 Commercial Fit 分離。

Abstract

The strengthened run observed 44 material candidate mentions, merged repeated identities into 34 canonical candidates, fully researched 20, qualified 15 before Audit, and downgraded three during mandatory skeptical review. The main contribution is not the final count; it is that role drift, duplicate inflation, and contact-policy errors were converted into explicit process checks.

強化版共觀察到 44 個 Material Candidate Mentions，透過 Canonical Dedup 收斂成 34 個 Unique Candidates，完整研究 20 家，其中 15 家先進入 QUALIFIED，Mandatory Audit 再將 3 家降級。核心價值不是最後數字，而是把 Role Drift、Duplicate Inflation 與 Contact-policy Error 變成明確的流程檢查。

I

Experiment design and process.

實驗設計與過程。

The user assumption was distributor-first, but the system was required to keep adjacent roles provisional until verified.

使用者初始假設偏向 Distributor，但系統必須把相鄰角色維持為 provisional，直到 Evidence 驗證。

Primary hypothesis

Importer / Distributor

Overseas-brand representation, wholesale reach, and category relevance were the core commercial tests.

核心檢查是 Overseas-brand Representation、Wholesale Reach 與 Category Relevance。

Explicit exclusions

Category relevance was not enough

Category Relevant 不等於合格 Prospect

Pure retailers, own-brand manufacturers without external-brand evidence, category-mismatched importers, and unverifiable directory entries were excluded or downgraded.

Pure Retailer、沒有 External-brand Evidence 的 Own-brand Manufacturer、Category Mismatch Importer 與無法驗證的 Directory Entry 都會被排除或降級。

II

Observed decision corrections.

實際發生的決策修正。

Prospect Audit materially changed the output rather than restating prior qualification.

Prospect Audit 真的改變了輸出，而不是替前一次 Qualification 重述理由。

Audit downgrade

Importer, but shallow demonstrated reach

有 Importer Role，但 Distribution Depth 不足

A genuine importer role did not automatically justify first-tier priority.

即使 Importer Role 為真，也不自動等於 First-tier Priority。

Audit downgrade

New company, unproven channel depth

新公司，Channel Reach 尚未證明

Business-model alignment was preserved, but priority was reduced until operating reach could be demonstrated.

Business-model Alignment 保留，但在 Operating Reach 尚未證明前降低優先級。

Contact policy

Commercial fit survived a blocked route

Contact Route 被封鎖，不代表 Commercial Fit 消失

One strong prospect remained QUALIFIED / AUDIT_PASS while its visible public form was marked DO_NOT_USE.

其中一個強 Prospect 保持 QUALIFIED / AUDIT_PASS，但公開表單被標記為 DO_NOT_USE。

IV

Final review: interpretation and limitations.

最終檢討：解讀與限制。

The case supports process reliability, not market-outcome prediction.

此案例支持的是 Process Reliability，不是 Market Outcome Prediction。

The process made it harder for category familiarity, company size, or contactability to substitute for actual commercial-role evidence.
流程降低了「品牌熟悉度、公司規模、找得到聯絡方式」取代真正 Commercial-role Evidence 的機會。

Limitations

限制

  • This was a regression, not a blind benchmark.
  • Exact product economics were intentionally incomplete.
  • Audit was performed by the same underlying model.
  • SHORTLIST_READY does not predict partnership or reply.
  • 這是 Regression，不是 Blind Benchmark。
  • Exact Product Economics 刻意不完整。
  • Audit 仍由同一底層模型執行。
  • SHORTLIST_READY 不等於會合作或回覆。
