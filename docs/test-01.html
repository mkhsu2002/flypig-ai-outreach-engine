Golden Test 01 · 日本戶外市場

LLM 跑很長之後，還能不能把商業角色分清楚？

Test 01 用真實日本戶外市場資料，壓測 role classification、canonical dedup、skeptical audit、contact-policy separation 與長流程一致性。

The setup.

A fictional Canadian outdoor-accessories brand explored Japan. The initial assumption was distributor-first, but the product and commercial details were intentionally incomplete.

測試設定。

一個虛構的加拿大戶外配件品牌準備探索日本市場。初始假設是 distributor-first，但 SKU、價格、margin、物流與定位刻意保持不完整。

Mission state

PARTIALLY_READY

Enough was known to research channel and organization fit, but not enough to pretend any company would buy the product.

已足以研究 channel / organization fit，但不足以宣稱任何公司一定會買。

Key risk

Role confusion

角色混淆

Outdoor search results can mix importers, distributors, retailers, manufacturers, agents, and market-entry operators.

戶外市場搜尋結果很容易把 importer、distributor、retailer、manufacturer、agency、market-entry operator 混在一起。

What v2 made mandatory.

The strengthened run was not just “research more carefully.” It introduced explicit gates that the same LLM had to follow.

v2 強制加入了什麼。

強化版不是單純叫 LLM「再仔細一點」，而是把幾個重要行為變成 mandatory gates。

Dedup

Repeated search hits became one canonical identity.

重複搜尋結果只能回到同一個 canonical identity。

44 material mentions collapsed to 34 unique canonical candidates before the full research set was chosen.

44 個 material mentions 經過 identity dedup 後，形成 34 個 unique canonical candidates。

Evidence

Business role had to be verified, not guessed.

Business role 必須驗證，不能用印象猜。

Retail relevance, company size, or keyword overlap could not substitute for import / wholesale / agency evidence.

戶外相關、公司很大、關鍵字很像，都不能代替 import / wholesale / agency 的 evidence。

Audit

Every QUALIFIED prospect had to survive a skeptical second pass.

每個 QUALIFIED prospect 都必須再接受 skeptical second pass。

15 prospects entered Audit; 3 were downgraded to SECONDARY.

15 家進入 Audit，最後有 3 家被降為 SECONDARY。

Route state

Commercial fit was separated from contactability.

Commercial fit 與 contactability 被拆開判斷。

One commercially strong account remained qualified while its visible public route was marked DO_NOT_USE.

有一家商業適配度很高的公司仍保持 QUALIFIED，但公開可見 route 被標成 DO_NOT_USE。

The audit changed real decisions.

The point was not to create an extra ceremonial review step. Audit materially changed the final Tracker.

Audit 真的改變了結果。

這不是多一個形式上的 review stage；Audit 實際改變了 final Tracker。

Downgrade 1

Distribution depth too weak

Distribution depth 不足

A genuine importer / wholesaler did not have enough evidence of first-tier distribution strength.

角色是真的 importer / wholesaler，但 evidence 不足以支持 first-tier priority。

Downgrade 2

Too new to infer reach

公司太新，不能直接推論 reach

Strong role alignment was not enough to prove mature execution capability.

角色很符合，但不能因此直接推論已經有成熟的 channel execution 能力。

Downgrade 3

Broad importer, weak outdoor depth

廣泛 importer，但 outdoor depth 太弱

General import capability was real, but specialist outdoor-channel evidence was not strong enough.

一般進口能力是真的，但 specialist outdoor-channel evidence 不夠強。

Final result.

The stronger output was not “more leads.” It was a smaller set with clearer role evidence, preserved rejections, and auditable decision changes.

最終結果。

更好的成果不是「更多 leads」，而是 role evidence 更清楚、rejected history 被保留、decision changes 可稽核。

What Test 01 established

Test 01 建立了什麼

A single capable LLM can become materially more reliable when role verification, dedup, skeptical audit, batch review, state semantics, and stop conditions are explicit process requirements.

只要 role verification、Dedup、skeptical Audit、batch review、state semantics、stop conditions 變成明確流程要求，同一個 capable LLM 的長流程可靠度可以明顯提升。
