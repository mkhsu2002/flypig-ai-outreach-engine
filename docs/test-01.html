Experiment 01 · Japan Outdoor Market

Can business roles stay accurate across a long prospect search?

長流程搜尋後，商業角色還能保持準確嗎？

A privacy-safe Canadian outdoor-accessories brand explored Japan. The test asked whether the process could keep importers, distributors, retailers, and manufacturers distinct while the candidate pool kept growing.

以匿名化的加拿大戶外配件品牌探索日本市場。這個實驗要測的是：當候選公司越找越多時，系統能否持續正確區分 Importer、Distributor、Retailer 與 Manufacturer。

01

Question and setup

題目與設定

Starting assumption

Distributor-first

The working hypothesis favored importers and distributors with overseas-brand representation, wholesale reach, and category relevance.

初始假設偏向有海外品牌代理、批發能力與品類相關性的 Importer / Distributor。

Main risk

Role drift

角色漂移

A long search can slowly admit retailers, own-brand manufacturers, or loosely related firms even when the mission never formally changed.

長流程搜尋很容易慢慢把 Retailer、Own-brand Manufacturer 或只是類別相關的公司也納入，最後偏離原始任務。

02

Process

過程

Every candidate passed through the same sequence before reaching the final shortlist.

每個候選對象都要經過同一套流程，才能進入最終名單。

01

Discover broadly

廣泛搜尋

Search across Japanese and English public sources.

使用日文與英文公開來源擴大候選範圍。

02

Canonical dedup

Canonical 去重

Merge aliases, repeated listings, and overlapping identities before research.

在正式研究前先合併別名、重複結果與重疊身份。

03

Research the role

查證商業角色

Check whether the company actually imports, distributes, represents, retails, or manufactures.

查證公司究竟是 Import、Distribution、Brand Representation、Retail 還是 Manufacturing。

04

Skeptical audit

反向 Audit

Re-read every qualified candidate and look for reasons to downgrade it.

所有 Qualified Candidate 都重新從反方立場檢查，尋找應降級的理由。

15 candidates initially qualified. Mandatory audit downgraded 3 of them before the final list.

15 家原本進入 QUALIFIED，但 Mandatory Audit 再把其中 3 家降級，最終留下 12 家。

04

What the experiment changed

實驗帶來的修正

Lesson 01

Importer does not automatically mean priority

Importer 不等於一定優先

One candidate had a genuine importer role but insufficient demonstrated distribution depth.

有公司確實是 Importer，但公開證據不足以支持高優先級的 Distribution Reach。

Lesson 02

A new company can fit but still remain secondary

商業模式吻合，仍可能只適合 Secondary

Fit was preserved, but operating reach was not yet proven strongly enough.

Business Model 雖然吻合，但實際 Channel Reach 尚未被充分證明。

Lesson 03

Contactability is separate from commercial fit

聯絡方式與商業適配是兩件事

A strong prospect remained qualified even when its visible public contact form was not appropriate for outreach.

即使公開 Contact Route 不適合使用，公司本身仍可能保持 Qualified。

05

Final review

最終心得與限制

The useful result was not “12 companies.” It was proving that category similarity, company size, and contactability did not automatically become evidence of the right commercial role.
真正重要的不是「找到 12 家」，而是流程能阻止類別相似、公司規模或找得到聯絡方式，直接被誤當成正確商業角色的證據。

Limitations

限制

  • This was a regression test, not a blind benchmark.
  • Exact product economics were intentionally incomplete.
  • The audit used the same underlying model.
  • SHORTLIST_READY does not predict replies or partnerships.
  • 這是 Regression Test，不是 Blind Benchmark。
  • 產品價格與完整商業條件刻意不完整。
  • Audit 仍由同一底層模型執行。
  • SHORTLIST_READY 不代表一定會回覆或合作。
