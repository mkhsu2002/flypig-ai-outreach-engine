Experiment 03 · Canada · Product-Market Discovery

We built the product. The harder question was: who should care first?

產品做出來了。更難的問題是：第一批到底該找誰？

Instead of choosing an ICP in advance, FlyPig used its own process to research who in Canada had the strongest workflow fit. Then we ran the same business problem through a fresh high-capability LLM without FlyPig's process rules.

這次沒有先指定 ICP。FlyPig 直接用自己的流程研究：加拿大哪些組織與這套產品的工作方式最吻合？之後再用一個全新的高階 LLM，不使用 FlyPig Process Rules，處理同一個商業問題。

ICP shiftICP 收斂BROAD_ADOPTER_DISCOVERY → SERVICE_MULTIPLIER_FIRST

The strongest first-wave targets became firms that repeatedly perform prospect research for clients, plus AI/CRM implementers that can reuse the workflow across many customers.

最值得優先接觸的對象，變成反覆替客戶進行 Prospect Research 的服務公司，以及能把這套流程重複導入不同客戶的 AI / CRM Implementer。

01

The business problem

商業問題

This is the same problem many new products face after launch: the product exists, but “who should we sell to first?” is still unresolved.

這其實就是很多新產品上線後會遇到的問題：產品已經存在，但「第一批到底該賣給誰？」仍然沒有答案。

Possible ICP

Direct users

直接使用者

Founders, sales teams, export teams, consultants, lead-generation firms, or market-entry providers could all plausibly use prospect research.

Founder、Sales Team、Export Team、Consultant、Lead-generation Firm、Market-entry Provider 都可能需要 Prospect Research。

Possible multiplier

AI / CRM implementers

AI / CRM Implementer

These firms could embed the workflow into CRM, qualification, research, or sales-automation projects across multiple clients.

這些公司可以把流程嵌入 CRM、Qualification、Research 或 Sales Automation 專案，並跨多個客戶重複使用。

Possible ecosystem

Communities and amplifiers

Community 與放大節點

Accelerators, training organizations, founder communities, and ecosystem channels can influence adoption even if they are not direct buyers.

Accelerator、Training Organization、Founder Community 與其他 Ecosystem Channel 即使不是直接買家，也可能大幅影響採用。

What the control added

Broader ecosystem thinking

更廣的 Ecosystem 思考

It considered accelerators, training organizations, founder communities, events, and other amplification channels more aggressively than the original FlyPig run.

它更積極地考慮 Accelerator、Training Organization、Founder Community、Event 與其他 Amplification Channel。

That was useful enough to change the product. FlyPig added a Discovery Diversity Check so an ambiguous campaign must test materially different routes before converging too early.
這個發現重要到足以修改產品。FlyPig 因此加入 Discovery Diversity Check，要求模糊任務在過早收斂之前，必須先測試實質不同的市場路徑。
05

The business takeaway

真正的商業心得

When a new product does not yet have a clear ICP, research should not merely “find companies.” It should help discover which type of customer, partner, or multiplier deserves the first sales conversation.
當一個新產品還沒有明確 ICP 時，研究不應只是「幫你找公司」，而應該幫你找出：哪一種客戶、合作夥伴或 Multiplier 最值得成為第一批商務對話對象。
What this result does not claim這個結果不代表什麼
The 13 shortlisted organizations were evidence-backed targets for a first adoption conversation. The experiment did not test actual replies, adoption, budget, or willingness to change workflow.
13 家 Shortlisted Organization 代表有足夠證據值得進行第一輪 Adoption Conversation；實驗沒有驗證實際回覆、採用、預算或改變 Workflow 的意願。
